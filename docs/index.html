BlinkBreak
iOS · Requires iOS 26

Rest your eyes every 20 minutes.

BlinkBreak enforces the 20-20-20 rule with a full-screen alarm that actually gets your attention.

The 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds. It's what eye doctors recommend for screen use — and the hardest part is remembering to do it.

Why a banner won't do

Every other break-reminder app sends a notification. You swipe it away and keep working. BlinkBreak uses Apple's AlarmKit: a full-screen takeover that plays at alarm volume, breaks through Focus and Do Not Disturb, and asks you to look away before it dismisses.

What's in it

  • 20-20-20 rule, enforced — not just suggested
  • Full-screen AlarmKit alerts that cut through Focus / silent mode
  • Optional weekly schedule — runs only during work hours
  • Custom alarm sound (or go silent)
  • "Take Break Now" for the midday headache moment
  • No account, no tracking, no network calls
