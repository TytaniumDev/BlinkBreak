Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

BlinkBreak does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, except what you choose to include when you send feedback.

Data stored on your device

BlinkBreak saves your session state (whether a break cycle is running, the current alarm ID, and your optional weekly schedule) in local app storage. This data never leaves your device.

Crash diagnostics

BlinkBreak uses Sentry to report anonymous crash and error diagnostics. These reports contain technical information about what went wrong (stack traces, iOS version, device model) and do not include personal data or app content.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app, your message, any email address you choose to include, and recent app logs are sent to the developer through Sentry. The email is used only to reply to you.

No analytics. No advertising. No account.
