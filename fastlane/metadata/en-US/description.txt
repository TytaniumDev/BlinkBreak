BlinkBreak enforces the 20-20-20 rule eye doctors recommend for screen use: every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Unlike a gentle banner you'll swipe away, BlinkBreak uses Apple's AlarmKit to deliver a full-screen alarm you actually notice — it plays at alarm volume regardless of silent switch, Focus, or Do Not Disturb. Tap "Start break", look away for 20 seconds, and get back to work.

• 20-20-20 rule, enforced — not just suggested
• Full-screen AlarmKit alerts break through Focus and silent mode
• Optional weekly schedule — runs only during work hours
• Custom alarm sound, or go silent
• "Take Break Now" for the midday headache moment
• No account, no tracking, no network calls — your data never leaves your device

Requires iOS 26 (AlarmKit).
